What we do

One team for all of your marketing, so you do not have to hire an editor, a designer, an ads specialist and a developer separately.

Based in Warsaw, clients across Poland and abroad. On-site shoots in Warsaw and Silesia, other cities by arrangement.

  • Social media

    We run Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube: content plan, posts, reels, stories and replies to messages.

    Script, voice-over, editing and captions. We can also work with footage you already have on your phone.

Questions

How much does working with ZOVU cost?

Reels cost 600 PLN a month on Start, 1000 PLN on Standard and 1500 PLN on Premium. The content autopilot starts at 399 PLN for the first month. Full marketing (social media, ads, website) is quoted individually. The first sample is free.

How fast can we start?

We deliver the free sample within 48 hours of our chat. The content autopilot goes live within 24 hours. Larger scopes, such as ads and running your profiles, start once we agree the plan.

Do I have to film videos or show my face?

No. We make reels from graphics, photos and material you already have, or from phone footage. If you want, we can shoot on site in Warsaw or Silesia, other cities by arrangement.

Where do you work?

We are based in Warsaw and work with businesses across Poland. We also have clients abroad, for example the Zurich brand New Orchard. Most of the work is done remotely.

Who have you worked with?

Among others TVN on the show Amorki, the Fight Mode galas, the brand New Orchard, the PickActive club, as well as MAG SHAURMA, MAYA GOLD, Bee Gelato and Bar Gucio.

What does day-to-day work look like?

We write, edit and publish, you approve. We talk on WhatsApp and you get a monthly report. Source files stay with you.

Do I sign a contract for a year?

No. The contract is monthly and you can end it at any time.
